Precision field instrument: the app should feel like an engineering calculator used on a job site—dense information, clear hierarchy, restrained surfaces, strong numeric readability, and a single solar-amber signal color. Light and dark modes. Tight dashboard pacing. Use crisp borders, modest radii, minimal shadows, tabular numerals, and a technical display face for headings paired with a quiet sans for controls and body text. Accent color is reserved for active calculations, key outputs, and primary actions; warnings and errors remain semantic. The visual language should communicate traceable engineering decisions rather than consumer-app decoration.
